How it feels

A quiet portal hiding inside ordinary things.

Revia stays out of the way until you reach for it. Point your camera at a linked image, and the memory unfolds — soft motion, real sound, no clutter.

    Scan the linked image

    Aim your camera at the printed image you linked a memory to.

  • 2
    Recognise the link

    Revia tracks the image and prepares its linked memory.

  • 3
    Step into the memory
